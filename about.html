About Crying Cloud

Crying Cloud is a mental health awareness project created to spread positivity and comfort to teenagers and young adults going through difficult times.

We believe that everyone deserves access to supportive resources and a safe space to express their emotions.

Our Story

Crying Cloud was born from a simple idea: that art and words can heal. We create digital artwork and comforting quotes to remind you that you are not alone in your struggles.

Who We Help

Audience How We Help
Students Coping with academic stress and pressure
Teenagers Navigating emotions and finding support
